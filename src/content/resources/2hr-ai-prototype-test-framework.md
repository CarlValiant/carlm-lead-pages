## What's Inside

Most ideas die inside a Notion doc. This guide forces them out into the market in a single afternoon.

- **The Loop** — the 5-step pipeline from "I have an idea" to "real strangers are clicking on real ads"
- **The Prompts** — paste-ready prompts for Manus self-prompting, Higgsfield image generation (Nano Banana 2 / GPT image 2), and Seedance 2.0 scene animation
- **The Ad Setup** — exact campaign objective, budget, audience, placements, and creative split for a 7-day Meta Ads test
- **Is It Actually Working?** — strict thresholds for CTR, CPC, landing-page conversion, and cost-per-lead so vibes can't override data
- **Run the Loop** — a print-and-tick checklist you can rip through in one sitting

## Who This Is For

Founders, indie builders, and operators who already have a list of ideas and are tired of researching instead of shipping. If you can write a one-line pitch and run a $50 ad budget, you can run this loop today.

## Why It Works

You are not trying to "validate the idea" with friends or surveys. You are paying real strangers' attention to find out whether the idea earns a click and an action. The framework makes that test cheap, fast, and disciplined enough that the result is actually trustworthy — not a vibe.
